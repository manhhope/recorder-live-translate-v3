## 1. Nguyen nhan (doc ma nguon cpal 0.18.2, wasapi 0.27, tauri 2.12.5)

- **xrun lap 14 lan**: `ErrorKind::Xrun` cua cpal WASAPI sinh tu co `AUDCLNT_BUFFERFLAGS_DATA_DISCONTINUITY`, la loi KHONG nghiem trong, stream van chay. Ma cu coi moi loi la mat thiet bi.
- **device_changed bao theo moi loi**: error callback map moi loi thanh DeviceChanged. Gio phan loai: Xrun chi dem, DeviceChanged/StreamInvalidated/DeviceNotAvailable moi dung lai stream.
- **mic bi coi im lang o 806 ms**: ben mic cham, MixedSource day ben he thong chay solo khi hang doi he thong qua 3 khung (300 ms) trong luc ben kia tut lai. Ghep FIFO nen mic den tre van duoc tron sau do (co test).
- **`Handle wait timed out`**: la het han cho binh thuong cua `wait_for_event` (50 ms) khi khong co du lieu, KHONG phai loi. Gio im lang; chi khi tra ve nhanh bat thuong (< 25 ms) moi log `wait_failed_fast` (gioi han 10 s/lan).
- **Chua chung minh duoc**: vi sao WASAPI gan co discontinuity ngay tu dau. Gia thuyet: I/O cham trong error callback duoi mutex toan cuc cua diag tren luong capture, bo dem mac dinh nho, cap phat trong callback. Can do tren Windows that.

## 3. ui_stall va js_silent (src-tauri)

- **js_silent=8000 o subtitle, quick-translate, capture-overlay-0: CANH BAO GIA.** `diag_js` khong co trong `APP_COMMANDS` va khong cap quyen o capability nao. Tauri 2.12.5 da co app manifest thi lenh app khong thuoc capability nao bi chan ("Command diag_js not allowed by ACL"), nen JS cua cua so phu khong bao gio toi Rust, den 8 s Rust bao im. Chu thich cu trong build.rs/diag.rs ("lenh khong co trong manifest thi ACL khong kiem") sai voi phien ban nay. Sua: them `diag_js` vao APP_COMMANDS, `allow-diag-js` vao overlay, quick-translate, quick-toolbar, capture-overlay, them `permissions/autogenerated/diag_js.toml`, test `diag_js_is_in_the_manifest_and_granted_to_every_instrumented_window`, noi long test cua so phu de cho rieng diag_js.
- **ui_stall silent_ms, session=idle, tu hoi phuc ~1 s: CANH BAO GIA sau khi may ngu.** Dong ho don dieu cua Windows khong dem luc ngu roi "bat kip", nen watchdog thay nhip UI cu qua 5 s. Sua: `classify_tick(wall_gap, mono_gap)` so dong ho tuong va don dieu cua chinh luong watchdog; neu lech > 2 s hoac nhip tre > 3 s thi ghi `suspend_detected`, xep lai moc, khong dump. UI treo that thi watchdog van nhip deu 1 s nen van bao `ui_stall` (co test).
- Han che: voi ui_stall chi co bang chung tu doc ma va test don vi; chua tai hien tren Windows ngu that.
