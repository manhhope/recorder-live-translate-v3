## Google Desktop app client co can client_secret khi doi token PKCE khong

Tai lieu Google ("OAuth 2.0 for Mobile & Desktop Apps", https://developers.google.com/identity/protocols/oauth2/native-app, doc ngay 2026-11-15): o buoc doi code lay token (`https://oauth2.googleapis.com/token`) va buoc refresh, `client_secret` ghi la **Optional**; ghi chu rieng: khong ap dung cho client loai Android, iOS, Chrome. Tai lieu cung noi client cai tren may khong giu bi mat duoc ("the client cannot keep the client_secret confidential").
Ket luan: theo tai lieu thi PKCE khong bat buoc secret, nhung loai client **Desktop app** van duoc Google cap san mot secret (khong coi la bi mat) va thuc te cac client nay hay bi token endpoint tu choi neu thieu (loi `client_secret is missing`). **Chua kiem bang client that** (khong co khoa tren may dev), nen dieu nay la nhan dinh, chua xac nhan bang thuc nghiem.
Chu can dua: **2 gia tri** de an toan (Client ID + Client secret cua client Desktop app); neu thu thieu secret ma van chay thi chi can 1 (Client ID). Code ho tro ca hai: client_secret chi gui khi co.
